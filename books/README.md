# 📚 Books

Books I've read and keep recommending. Most of them are about software architecture, modeling and teams. A few have nothing to do with code at all.

Every book has its own page with my take and what I took away from it.

## 🧩 Software Architecture

- **[Fundamentals of Software Architecture](fundamentals-of-software-architecture.md)** · Mark Richards, Neal Ford<br>
  The book I'd hand to anyone who is moving into architecture.
- **[Head First Software Architecture](head-first-software-architecture.md)** · Raju Gandhi, Mark Richards, Neal Ford<br>
  Lighter than its siblings, with puzzles, pictures and plenty of humor.
- **[Software Architecture: The Hard Parts](software-architecture-the-hard-parts.md)** · Neal Ford, Mark Richards, Pramod Sadalage, Zhamak Dehghani<br>
  This book starts where most microservice talks stop.
- **[Building Micro-Frontends](building-micro-frontends.md)** · Luca Mezzalira<br>
  An honest book about micro-frontends.
- **[Get Your Hands Dirty on Clean Architecture](get-your-hands-dirty-on-clean-architecture.md)** · Tom Hombergs<br>
  Short, practical and full of Java code.
- **[Residues](residues.md)** · Barry O'Reilly<br>
  A book that changed how I think about architecture.
- **[The Architect's Paradox](the-architects-paradox.md)** · Barry O'Reilly<br>
  Philosophy for software architects sounds strange at first, but it works surprisingly well.

## 🗺️ Domain & Process Modeling

- **[Learning Domain-Driven Design](learning-domain-driven-design.md)** · Vlad Khononov<br>
  The most approachable introduction to DDD I know.
- **[Praxishandbuch BPMN](praxishandbuch-bpmn.md)** · Jakob Freund, Bernd Rücker<br>
  My go-to book for BPMN.
- **[BPMN 2.0 – Business Process Model and Notation](bpmn-2-0-allweyer.md)** · Thomas Allweyer<br>
  A compact and precise introduction to the standard.

## 👥 Teams & Delivery

- **[Team Topologies](team-topologies.md)** · Matthew Skelton, Manuel Pais<br>
  This book gave me the vocabulary for conversations about team structures.
- **[Accelerate](accelerate.md)** · Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble, Gene Kim<br>
  Finally numbers instead of opinions.

## 🌌 Beyond Software

- **[Everything Is F\*cked](everything-is-fucked.md)** · Mark Manson<br>
  Less self-help, more philosophy.
- **[Kurze Antworten auf große Fragen](kurze-antworten-auf-grosse-fragen.md)** · Stephen Hawking<br>
  Big questions, short answers and a lot of curiosity.
