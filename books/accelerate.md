## 💭 My take

Finally numbers instead of opinions. The book backs DevOps practices with solid research, which makes it a strong argument in any discussion about delivery.

## 💡 What I took away

Speed and stability aren't a trade-off, because good teams get both. The four key metrics are a simple way to see where a team actually stands.
