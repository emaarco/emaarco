## 💭 My take

A book that changed how I think about architecture. Instead of predicting the future, Barry O'Reilly stresses a design with random events and keeps what survives.

## 💡 What I took away

Nobody knows what's going to happen. But we can build systems that survive things we didn't expect, and that's a far more honest starting point than a list of requirements.
