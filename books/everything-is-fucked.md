## 💭 My take

Less self-help, more philosophy. Manson mixes Kant, Nietzsche and psychology in his usual direct style, and somehow it works.

## 💡 What I took away

Hope keeps us going, but it also makes us vulnerable. The book made me think about what I actually believe in, and why.
