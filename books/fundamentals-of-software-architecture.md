## 💭 My take

The book I'd hand to anyone who is moving into architecture. It doesn't sell a favorite style. Instead, it maps the whole field in a calm and structured way.

## 💡 What I took away

There is no best architecture, only the least worst one for your context. So I start with the characteristics that really matter, and only then talk about styles.
