## 💭 My take

This book starts where most microservice talks stop. Instead of best practices, it hands you trade-off analyses, and that's exactly what real projects need.

## 💡 What I took away

Splitting a system is easy. Splitting its data and transactions is the hard part, and that thought followed me straight into distributed transactions with process engines.
