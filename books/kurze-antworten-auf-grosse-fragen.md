## 💭 My take

Big questions, short answers and a lot of curiosity. Hawking explains physics in a way that's easy to follow without ever feeling shallow.

## 💡 What I took away

Thinking about black holes and the origin of the universe puts everyday problems into perspective. It also reminded me how much fun it is to simply wonder.
