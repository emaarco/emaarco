## 💭 My take

The most approachable introduction to DDD I know. Khononov connects the strategic and the tactical side without getting lost in theory.

## 💡 What I took away

Not every part of a system deserves the same effort. Telling core from supporting subdomains, and local from global complexity, still shapes how I look at systems.
