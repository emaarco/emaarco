## 💭 My take

This book gave me the vocabulary for conversations about team structures. Four team types and three interaction modes sound simple, and that's exactly the point.

## 💡 What I took away

Cognitive load is a real design constraint. If a team has to know too much, the architecture will show it sooner or later.
