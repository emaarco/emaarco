## 💭 My take

Philosophy for software architects sounds strange at first, but it works surprisingly well. It's the theoretical companion to Residues and a slow, rewarding read.

## 💡 What I took away

Many of our habits as architects rest on beliefs we never questioned. Since reading it, I notice how much uncertainty we tend to hide behind clean diagrams.
