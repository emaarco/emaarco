## 💭 My take

Short, practical and full of Java code. It's the kind of book that turns hexagonal architecture from a diagram into something you can actually build.

## 💡 What I took away

The domain sits in the center, and everything else stays at the edge, frameworks and process engines included. I use that idea almost daily, for example to keep business logic out of the engine.
