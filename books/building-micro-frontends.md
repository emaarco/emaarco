## 💭 My take

An honest book about micro-frontends. It shows where they help, but also where a well-structured monolith is simply the better choice.

## 💡 What I took away

Micro-frontends are an organizational decision first and a technical one second. If the team boundaries don't fit, no framework will fix it.
