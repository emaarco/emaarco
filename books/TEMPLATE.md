# Book entry template

Copy the block below into the matching section of [`README.md`](README.md) and replace the `UPPER_CASE` values.

- `BOOK_URL`: a freely accessible book or publisher page.
- `COVER_URL`: a stable cover image, e.g. `https://covers.openlibrary.org/b/isbn/ISBN-M.jpg`. Without a reliable cover, use `covers/placeholder.svg`.
- Keep `width="96"` and `align="left"` so all entries look the same.

```markdown
<a href="BOOK_URL"><img src="COVER_URL" alt="Cover of TITLE by AUTHORS" width="96" align="left"></a>

**[TITLE](BOOK_URL)**<br>
AUTHORS · PUBLISHER, EDITION, YEAR

**My take:** OPINION

**What I took away:** LEARNINGS

<br clear="left">
```
