## 💭 My take

Lighter than its siblings, with puzzles, pictures and plenty of humor. It's a great entry point, and still a nice refresher if you've already read Fundamentals.

## 💡 What I took away

Architecture is a series of decisions, and decisions deserve to be written down. After this book, architecture decision records felt less like paperwork and more like common sense.
